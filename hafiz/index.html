Quran Guardians

Read and memorise the Quran.

A free app for reading the Quran and learning it by heart, with recitations, a short tafsir, hadiths and prayer times: no ads, no account, nothing collected. In eight languages.

Memorise, part by part

Short parts of about ten verses: hide the words, tap to reveal them, then test yourself.

Practise

Review that comes back on time

A memorised part returns for review after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60 and 120 days; a part you forgot comes back tomorrow.
