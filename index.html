02 / How we build

Small team.
Clear principles.

Whatever the topic, we build every Vatani Tech app by the same four principles.

Offline whenever possible

We do our best to make our apps work without a signal: on a rig, at a mine or on a plane.

Simple and focused

One clear purpose per app, no clutter, readable at any text size.

Accurate content

Carefully prepared, checked against sources, and improved with every report.

Privacy respected

No unnecessary data. What you write stays on your phone unless you choose otherwise.
